Har mahine 15 enquiry, admission sirf 3? Galti kahan ho rahi hai?

Zyadatar coaching centres har enquiry ke saath ek jaisa behave karte hain: phone pe fees bata di, aur phir wait. Lekin har enquiry alag hoti hai: Hot, Warm ya Cold. Aur har ek ko alag follow-up chahiye.

इस reel में step by step समझिए:
1. Pehli call pe fees se pehle 3 sawaal: kab se join karna hai, bacche ko kya dikkat hai, is hafte centre aa sakte hain?
2. Jawab se decide karo: Hot, Warm ya Cold
3. Hot enquiry ko phone pe sab mat batao, centre bulao: "10 minute mein sab samjha doonga"
4. Jaane se pehle agla step fix karo: trial ki date
5. Warm ko pressure nahi, value do: har 3–4 din result, batch date ya study tip
6. Cold ke peeche mat bhaago: mahine mein 1 message
7. Number aise save karo: H Rohan 9th, W Priya 10th, C Aman 8th. Admission ho gaya? ADM likh do

Coaching chalate ho ya padhate ho? Isse save karo aur us teacher dost ko bhejo jiske paas enquiry aati hai par admission nahi.

Poora enquiry tracking sheet chahiye to comment mein "ENQUIRY" likho.
