# Day 001 script for recording (bold kinetic text, about 45 s)

The same advice as your 2-minute voiceover, cut to short lines. **Each numbered line becomes one screen of big type**, so read them as written.

## How to record (phone is fine)
- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings (a bedroom beats an empty hall). Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little below it.
- Record the whole script in **one take**. Energy up, like you're telling one coaching owner a secret.
- **Leave a clear 1-second pause between numbered lines.** I use those pauses to time the text to your voice, so they matter more than speed.
- If you fluff a line, pause, then say the whole line again. I'll keep the last take.
- Send the file as it is (voice memo, .m4a, .mp3, .wav, or a video). No need to clean it up.

## Script

1. हर महीने 15 enquiry... admission सिर्फ़ 3?
2. गलती ये है: आप हर enquiry को एक जैसा treat करते हो।
3. पहली call पे fees मत बताओ। पहले 3 सवाल पूछो।
4. एक: कब से join करना है?
5. दो: बच्चे को किस चीज़ में दिक्कत है?
6. तीन: क्या इस हफ़्ते centre आ सकते हैं?
7. जवाब से तय करो: Hot, Warm या Cold।
8. Hot: centre बुलाओ, और जाने से पहले trial की date fix करो।
9. Warm: pressure नहीं, value दो। हर 3–4 दिन कुछ काम का भेजो।
10. Cold: पीछे मत भागो। महीने में बस एक message।
11. Number ऐसे save करो: H Rohan 9th. Admission हो गया? ADM लिख दो।
12. तीन सवाल। तीन group। तीन अलग follow-up।
13. Day 2 में: fees time पे कैसे आए? Follow कर लो।

About 115 words, or 40–50 s at a lively pace.

## What changed from your 2-minute script
- Kept: the hook, the 3 questions, Hot/Warm/Cold, the Hot steps (centre visit + trial date), the Warm and Cold follow-ups, the H/W/C/ADM saving trick, the recap, and the Day 2 tease.
- Cut for pace: "Hot: 2 hafte mein join…" definitions, "bharosa banta hai", "Hot ko zyada der mat rokna", the weekly H/W search tip, and "kai cold parents baad mein khud aate hain".
- Lines 1, 7 and 12 are written to land as single big screens, which is the strongest moment for this style.
