Scope: manages 1 YouTube channel and 4–5 Instagram accounts.

## 1. Role Summary

The Social Media Manager (SMM) owns day-to-day content planning, posting, community management, and reporting across:

- YouTube: `[ 1 channel name/link ]`
- Instagram: `[ list all 4-5 handles + what each account is for, e.g. main brand, founder personal, product line, region-specific, UGC/community ]`

## 2. Access & Security (do this before anything else)

Never share raw passwords. Use one of these, in order of preference:

1. **Meta Business Suite / Business Manager** — add the SMM as an *Employee* or *Admin* (per account) under Business Settings → People, scoped only to the Instagram accounts they manage. This avoids sharing the Instagram password at all.
2. **YouTube Studio** — add them as a **Manager** or **Editor** (not Owner) via YouTube Studio → Settings → Permissions, or through Google Workspace/Brand Account if you use one.
3. If an account has no Business Manager setup yet, migrate it first — do not hand out the personal login/password.
4. Set up **2FA on every account** using an authenticator app (not SMS) before granting access. Keep recovery codes with the owner, not the employee.
5. Maintain a private access log (who has access to what, since when) — a simple spreadsheet is fine. Revoke access immediately when someone leaves.

## 3. Brand Voice & Guardrails

Give the SMM a one-page brand sheet, not a 40-page brand book. Minimum contents:

- 3–5 adjectives describing the brand's tone (e.g. "direct, warm, a little funny — never sarcastic at customers")
- Words/topics to avoid
- Approved emoji/hashtag style (or none)
- Who to tag / never tag
- What requires approval before posting vs. what can go out independently (see §6)
- Escalation contact for anything reputational (negative viral comment, PR issue, legal question)

## 5. Content Calendar & Cadence

Set explicit minimums so "I'll post when inspired" doesn't happen:

- YouTube: `[ e.g. 1 long-form/week + 2 Shorts/week ]`
- Each Instagram account: `[ e.g. 3 feed posts/week, 5 Stories/week, 1-2 Reels/week ]`

Process:
1. SMM drafts the following week's calendar every Thursday/Friday (platform, account, format, caption draft, asset status).
2. Owner/approver reviews by end of day Friday (see §6 for what needs approval).
3. Content is scheduled at least 2 days ahead — no same-day scrambling unless it's timely/reactive content.
4. Repurpose aggressively: one piece of long-form YouTube content should seed 3–5 pieces of Instagram content (clips, quote graphics, carousel breakdown) rather than creating everything from scratch per platform.

---

## 6. Approval Workflow

Define this explicitly — it's the #1 source of friction otherwise.

- **Post without approval:** routine scheduled content that follows the brand sheet, Stories, community replies using approved FAQ answers.
- **Needs approval before posting:** anything mentioning pricing/promotions, responses to negative press or complaints, anything tagging a partner/other brand, any paid collaboration, any first-time content format.
- **Approver:** `[ name / role ]`, response SLA `[ e.g. within 24h ]`.

## 7. Community Management

- Response time target: comments and DMs answered within `[ e.g. 24 hours, business days ]`.
- Provide a short FAQ / canned-response doc for common questions (shipping, pricing, hours, etc.) so responses stay consistent across 5 accounts.
- Escalate immediately (don't attempt to handle solo): threats, legal complaints, harassment reports, anything that could go viral negatively, press inquiries.
- Standard practice: hide/delete spam and abusive comments per platform policy; never delete legitimate criticism — respond to it.

---

## 8. Reporting & KPIs

Monthly report (or weekly if the business is fast-moving), one page per platform:

**Per Instagram account:**
- Follower growth (net, not just gross)
- Reach / impressions
- Engagement rate (likes+comments+shares / reach)
- Top 3 performing posts and why (format, topic, timing)
- DMs/comments volume and response time

**YouTube:**
- Subscribers gained
- Views, average view duration, CTR on thumbnails
- Top 3 videos and why
- Comments volume and response time

Keep a running spreadsheet/Notion doc so trends are visible month over month, not just isolated snapshots.

---

## 9. First 2 Weeks — Suggested Ramp-Up

- **Days 1–2:** Access setup (§2), read brand sheet (§3), audit all 5 accounts' current state (bio, highlights, pinned content, recent performance).
- **Days 3–5:** Shadow/review last 3 months of posts per account to internalize tone and what has worked. Draft first week's content calendar for review.
- **Week 2:** Take over live posting and community management with daily check-ins with the owner/approver. Ship first monthly report template (even with partial data) to confirm format works.
