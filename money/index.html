          Import your bank statement

          1. Open your bank's app or net banking and go to account statements.
          2. Download the statement as CSV or Excel for the last month or two. If it's Excel, open it and save as CSV.
          3. Choose the file below, or paste its contents. Cards work the same way.

          The page finds the date, description and amount columns on its own, sorts each line into a category by the merchant name, and skips lines you've already imported. You can change any category afterwards.

          Live bank connection

          A page like this can't log in to your bank by itself, and you should never type your bank password into one. Live connections go through a regulated data service that your bank works with:

          • India: the RBI Account Aggregator network (apps like Finvu, OneMoney, or the aggregator option inside Fi, Jupiter and INDmoney). You approve read-only access to your accounts with OTP consent.
          • US, Canada, UK, EU: Plaid, TrueLayer or GoCardless Bank Account Data.

          Wiring one of these into this dashboard needs a small private server to hold the API keys. Until then, a statement import once a week takes about a minute and gives the coach everything it needs.
